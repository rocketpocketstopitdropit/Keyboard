wordlist.txt is generated during the build by scripts/make_wordlist.py from
wordfreq (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer.

wordfreq's word frequency data is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

It is built from Wikipedia, Subtitles (OpenSubtitles and SUBTLEX), News
(NewsCrawl and GDELT), Books (Google Books Ngrams), Web text (OSCAR),
Twitter, and Reddit. See the wordfreq README for the full list of sources
and their own licenses.
