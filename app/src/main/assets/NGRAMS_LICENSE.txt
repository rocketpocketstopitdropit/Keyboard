ngrams.txt is generated by scripts/make_ngrams.py from the English sentences
of Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), a collection of sentences and
translations contributed by volunteers.

Tatoeba sentences are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France
(CC BY 2.0 FR): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

ngrams.txt holds only counts of which words follow which; it contains no
sentences.
